Recycling instructions

  1. Back up important data, sign out of accounts, and factory-reset phones, tablets, and computers when possible.
  2. Remove SIM and memory cards. Do not share passwords or personal data with collection staff.
  3. Keep batteries separate where the centre requests it. Do not puncture, crush, or place damaged batteries in household waste.
  4. Do not dismantle devices or burn cables. Bring items to an authorised centre and follow its handling instructions.
  5. Ask for a receipt or tracking reference so you can follow the processing status.
